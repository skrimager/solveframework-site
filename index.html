The Method

The SOLVE Framework™

A five-step approach that replaces pressure with understanding — the foundation for both the books and every scenario inside the training platform.

S

Situation

Understand the situation before you say a word. Who is this person, why are they here, and what is really going on beneath the request?

O

Open with Questions

Lead with curiosity, not a pitch. The first move is a question, not a presentation.

L

Listen for the Outcome

Hear the future they are actually trying to reach. The product is never the point; the outcome is.

V

Visualize Success

Help them picture what "solved" looks like, so the decision feels clear instead of risky.

E

Engineer the Solution

Build the answer around them, even when the honest answer isn't you. That is what earns trust for life.
