YOUR BEST CONSULTANT.

Production tells you something important. It doesn't tell you everything.

  • Your top producer may work more leads.
  • They may have more tenure.
  • They may possess exceptional product knowledge.
  • They may have a larger existing book of business.
  • They may have stronger referral relationships.
  • They may simply make more calls or work more hours.

Meanwhile, another person may be exceptional at discovering what customers actually need, earning trust, handling difficult situations, solving problems, overcoming resistance, or creating experiences that generate referrals.
