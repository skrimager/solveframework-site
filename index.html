Objection Handling, the SOLVE Way

Objections aren't obstacles to overcome. They're diagnostics.

An objection is a signal that a step got skipped. "Let me think about it" usually means the real motivation never surfaced. "Too expensive" is a Visualize Success gap. SOLVE trains reps to hear which step an objection points to and return to it, like an advocate, not an opponent, instead of pushing a comeback line that closes nobody. The research backs it: top performers get fewer objections because of better discovery, not better rebuttals.

The 30-second version of practice.

Not everyone has 12 to 15 minutes a day to run a full practice conversation. That's real, and it's why most training programs quietly die.

But everyone has 30 seconds. Clip a call transcript, paste it in, and get it scored. That's it. Your rep just got the same rubric-based coaching they'd get from a full practice session, on a conversation they were already having anyway.

That means the reps who swear they don't have time still get coached. And it means the practice never stops just because the calendar got full.

For managers: this is a coaching tool, not a monitoring tool.

Let's be clear about what this is for. Uploading real conversations isn't about looking over anyone's shoulder or building a case against a rep. It's about finally being able to coach with something more than a hunch.

Right now, most coaching sounds like “you need to ask better questions.” That's not coaching, that's a wish. When a real conversation comes back scored against the same five steps, naming exactly where it stalled, the conversation changes to “here's the moment they told you business was slow, and here's what asking one more question would have opened up.” That's specific, it's teachable, and the rep can actually do something with it.

Every score and every word of feedback goes to the rep, always. Managers see the trends. Reps see how to get better. That's the standard we score them on, so it's the standard we hold ourselves to.
