Methodology & Real Call Scoring

Real Conversation Scoring

Upload a real call, a transcript, or a text thread. Get scored by the same rubric, not a random AI opinion.

Every other platform grades rehearsal. SOLVE also grades the real thing. Upload an actual call recording, paste a call transcript, or paste a real text or email exchange with a customer, and SOLVE Coach™ scores it against the exact same five-step SOLVE rubric your team certifies on, the same step-by-step breakdown, the same coaching, and a named diagnosis of precisely where the conversation stalled.

This is not generic AI feedback. Ask ChatGPT to grade a sales call and you'll get a different opinion every time you ask, a grader with no standard, no sequence, and no memory of your last score. SOLVE Coach™ measures every conversation, practiced or real, against one fixed standard: the published SOLVE Framework. An 85 means the same thing on a Tuesday phone call as it does on a certification exam. That's not a grade. That's a measurement system.

📞

Real calls, transcripts, texts, and emails

Audio recordings are transcribed and scored automatically. Already have a transcript from your phone system, Zoom, or dealer CRM? Paste it straight in, along with any text or email thread. Up to 20 real conversations scored per seat, every month, included with every seat, at no extra cost.

🎯

"Where it stalled"

Every scored conversation names the earliest SOLVE step that broke down, so coaching starts with a diagnosis, not a debate. When practice scores and field scores sit side by side on your dashboard, you're not wondering whether the practice transfers. You're watching it transfer.

🤝

Coaching, not surveillance

Every score and every word of feedback goes to the rep, always. Managers see the trends; reps see how to get better. Advocates, not opponents. That's the standard we score them on, so it's the standard we hold ourselves to.

Platforms grade the dress rehearsal. SOLVE grades opening night, with the same rubric, the same coach, and the same standard.
