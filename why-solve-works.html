The Method

The SOLVE Framework™

A five-step, consultative approach to discovery and leadership that replaces pressure with trust - the foundation for every Conversation inside the SOLVE Platform™, and for both books.

S

Situation

Understand the situation before you say a word. Who is this person, why are they here, and what is really going on beneath the request?

O

Open with Questions

Lead with curiosity, not a pitch. The first move is a question, not a presentation.

L

Listen for the Outcome

Hear the future they are actually trying to reach. The product is never the point; the outcome is.

V

Visualize Success

Help them picture what "solved" looks like, turning it into a shared goal that prioritizes their needs and wants, so the decision feels clear instead of risky.

E

Engineer the Solution

Build the answer around them, even when the honest answer isn't you. That is what earns trust for life.

Why SOLVE Is Different

Every AI can simulate a conversation. SOLVE teaches you how to think during one.

Instead of memorizing scripts or drilling objection handling, professionals learn how to genuinely understand the person in front of them, so the right solution becomes obvious to everyone.

Most practice tools measure whether you followed a script. SOLVE measures whether you actually understood the person you were talking to. At each step, the goal isn't compliance with a checklist, it's genuine comprehension: grasping the real situation before offering anything, surfacing the motivations behind the request, confirming the outcome they're actually after, helping them picture what "solved" looks like, and only then building a solution they can move forward on with confidence.

That shift, from reciting the right words to doing the right thinking, is what separates SOLVE from a script or a chatbot drill. The result is better conversations, stronger relationships, and more confident decisions. Because great consultants don't overcome objections, they reduce the need for them.

Not Random. Engineered.

These aren't random AI chats. Every conversation is engineered.

Every practice conversation on the SOLVE Platform™ is designed with a defined ideal outcome before your rep ever says a word. The AI customer has a real situation, real motivations, and doors that only open when your rep earns them through trust and the right questions. SOLVE Coach™ scores every session against that engineered outcome using the five-step SOLVE rubric, not against a script, and not on a whim.

The 85% gate

A rep who never uncovers the customer's real motivation cannot score above 85%, no matter how smooth they sounded. They retake it until the thinking is right.

Closer to the ideal, higher the score

The score measures the distance between the solution your rep engineered and the best possible solution for that customer.

Sometimes the highest-scoring answer is a referral

In some scenarios, the best outcome is referring the client to someone else. SOLVE scores judgment and integrity, not just closing, because forced deals become chargebacks and bad reviews.

Objection Handling, the SOLVE Way

Objections aren't obstacles to overcome. They're diagnostics.

Yes, the SOLVE Platform™ teaches objection handling, but not the way script libraries do. We don't hand your reps comeback lines, because "let me think about it" was never the real problem. An objection is a signal that a step got skipped. When a customer says "I need to talk to my wife" or "let me sleep on it," something upstream was missed, the real motivation never surfaced, or success was never truly visualized. A customer who can clearly picture what "solved" looks like has usually already had that conversation at home. That's why they're standing in your store.

So SOLVE trains reps to do what advocates do, not opponents: agree with the objection, then diagnose it. "I think you should talk it over, and before you do, let's make sure we've nailed down exactly what success looks like for you, so that conversation is an easy one." The rep returns to the step that got missed and earns the trust that was never established, instead of pushing a line that closes nobody and burns a customer who would have come back.

Every objection maps to a step

"Too expensive" is a Visualize Success gap. "Let me think about it" is a missed motivation. "I need to check with my partner" means the outcome was never made real. Reps learn to hear which step the objection points to, and return to it.

Practiced, not memorized

AI customers in Intermediate and Advanced scenarios raise real objections, stalls, spouse checks, price pushback, competitor comparisons. Reps practice diagnosing and recovering, scored against the SOLVE rubric like everything else.

Advocate, not opponent

Scripts create pressure; pressure creates walkaways and one-star reviews. Reps who respond to objections with agreement and better discovery close the deals worth closing, and get referred by the ones they don't.

This isn't theory. It's documented.

Research analyzing 35,000+ sales calls found top performers received fewer objections, because of superior discovery, not better comebacks. Many objections are created by the seller pitching before understanding. (Neil Rackham, SPIN Selling, Huthwaite research)

Telling someone they're free to say no roughly doubles compliance rates, confirmed across 40+ studies with more than 22,000 participants. Affirming autonomy beats applying pressure. (Carpenter, "But You Are Free" meta-analysis, 2013)

Customers who vividly imagine using a solution show significantly higher purchase intent. If they can't picture "solved," they haven't decided. (Gregory, Cialdini & Carpenter, 1982; supported by decades of customer-orientation research, Franke & Park, 2006)

Script-based selling overcomes objections. SOLVE-based selling makes most of them never come up, and diagnoses the ones that do.
