If you are a certified consultant, a licensed organization, or a customer of the SOLVE Framework platform, you may:

  • Refer to your own certification honestly and accurately, for example, "SOLVE Framework Certified Consultant" or "Certified through the SOLVE Academy."
  • Use the badge, coin, or certificate imagery issued to you by SOLVE Framework to represent your own earned status (e.g., on a business card, email signature, or LinkedIn profile), unaltered and without implying a broader partnership, endorsement, or license than what you actually hold.
  • Reference SOLVE Framework by name in factual, non-misleading statements about your training or certification (e.g., "trained using the SOLVE Framework").

You may not, without our prior written permission:
