SOLVE vs. Everyone Else

We don't just tell you who closed. We measure how well your people actually understand the customer, where they're strong, where they need to grow, and why they close when they do, the kind of thing most platforms can't measure with any consistency. Then we build a coach around each rep that targets exactly what they need to improve.

AI Practice Platforms Built on Sales Methodologies

What this category does well: real-call scoring on the same scorecard as practice, built for structured sales floors, often well-funded and mature.

Where SOLVE is different: these platforms typically score conversations against a sales methodology, SPIN, MEDDIC, Challenger, often several stitched together in one system. Mixing methodologies into one scoring engine creates inconsistency, an 85 doesn't mean the same thing twice. SOLVE runs on one proven, published method, so a score always means exactly what it says, and it measures whether the rep found the customer's real motivation, not just whether they followed a process.

Candidate Screening Tools

What this category does well: evaluating a candidate's conversational ability before hiring, proof this is a real, wanted capability.

Where SOLVE is different: some tools in this space set team-size minimums that exclude smaller businesses. SOLVE doesn't. And candidate screening runs on the same consistent, motivation-first rubric as everything else in the platform, not a separate methodology-based scorecard.

ChatGPT / generic AI chat

What it does well: flexible, cheap, available now.

Where SOLVE is different: no consistent rubric (ask it twice, get two different answers), no team-wide comparison, no certification, nothing tracked over time.

Coaching Is a Conversation

SOLVE Coach isn't a stand-alone advice tool that gives you an answer and sends you on your way. You can challenge the feedback, ask why it scored something the way it did, request clarification, and keep the conversation going until the coaching makes sense.

The One Thing Nobody Else Does

Every competitor's answer to a skill gap is more solo AI practice. SOLVE tells you who on your own team is already great at that exact thing, so you can pair them up and save yourself the coaching time. Their answer to a gap is more software. Ours is your own best people.
