Book Three · Conflict Management Tactics That Work

Stay Calm and Resolve

Tension is part of every business relationship, and how you handle it decides whether trust grows or quietly erodes. Stay Calm and Resolve extends the discovery-first thinking of the SOLVE Framework into conflict, showing you how to stay composed, understand what is really driving the friction, and guide the conversation toward a resolution both sides can stand behind. It gives you a repeatable approach you can practice before the moment arrives, so difficult conversations become chances to deepen the relationship.

  • Stay composed when a conversation turns tense or emotional
  • Uncover what is really driving the friction before you respond
  • Guide both sides toward a resolution that protects the relationship
  • Practice high-stakes conflict scenarios before they happen for real
